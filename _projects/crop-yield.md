## Overview

My undergraduate thesis applied state-of-the-art machine learning to predict crop yield for Ethiopian agricultural regions — a high-impact problem where accurate forecasts aid food security planning.

## Dataset

Curated a dataset from multiple sources: satellite imagery indices (NDVI), weather station records, soil composition data, and historical yield records. Combined automated scripting (Python + Pandas) with manual verification and STATA for statistical analysis.

## Model

Trained and evaluated several ML models, with a final **TensorFlow Deep Learning predictor** (MLP with attention over feature groups) outperforming classical baselines (Random Forest, XGBoost) on held-out test regions.

## Publication

Results and methodology published in the **Addis Ababa University Journal of Engineering**. This was among the first deep learning applications to this specific regional crop yield problem.
